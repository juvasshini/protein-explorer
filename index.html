1JNK · JNK3 kinase
A stress-activated kinase found mainly in the brain, linked to neuronal death in Alzheimer's, Parkinson's and stroke. Its ATP pocket is almost identical to JNK1 and JNK2, which makes selective drugs hard to design. I worked with JNK3 during my research internship at NUS.

1LYZ · lysozyme
An enzyme in tears, saliva and egg white that breaks down bacterial cell walls. One of the first enzyme structures ever solved, and a favourite test protein for crystallisation.
Experimental · X-ray
4HHB · haemoglobin
Carries oxygen in red blood cells using four subunits that work together. A single change in the β chain (glutamate 6 to valine) causes sickle-cell disease.
Experimental · X-ray
1ATP · protein kinase A
A classic model for how kinases hold ATP. Its fold is shared by hundreds of human kinases, including JNK3, so try loading both and comparing them.
Experimental · X-ray
6LU7 · SARS-CoV-2 main protease
The enzyme that cuts the virus's proteins into working pieces, solved in 2020 with an inhibitor bound. It's the target of the COVID-19 drug Paxlovid.
